Thank You

Your gift is a steadying light

Thank you for giving to the Aloka Foundation. Your generosity becomes something specific and real: a ramp, a semester, a set of adaptive tools, a neighborhood project. Because of you, we can say “yes” when the next need arrives.

What Happens Next

A receipt is on its way

Your emailed receipt comes from Zeffy, the donation platform we use so that 100% of your gift reaches our mission. If it hasn’t arrived within a few minutes, please check your spam folder.

  • Your gift goes directly to the Aloka Foundation
  • Monthly donors join our circle of Lightkeepers, whose steady support lets us move quickly when a need arises
  • You can change or cancel a monthly gift any time through the link in your receipt
