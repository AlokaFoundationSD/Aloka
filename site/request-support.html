Request Support

Submitting a formal request for support

The Aloka Foundation partners with people on specific, meaningful needs: financial support for home or vehicle accommodations, help with a specific product purchase, assistance with tuition or continuing education opportunities, and more. Tell us about your need and how it aligns with our mission.

Submitting opens your email app with your request ready to send. If you prefer, you can email us directly at kirk@lifeworks-sls.com. Our team reviews every request with care. We will follow up with your point of contact to learn more and to take the next steps together.
