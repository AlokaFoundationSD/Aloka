Where It Goes

Directly to real, specific needs

Government-funded services cover the essentials of daily support, and nothing more. Aloka funds what falls outside those lines: the wheelchair-accessible vehicle, the home modification, the tuition payment, the adaptive equipment, the neighborhood project.

  • Scholarships for individuals and for the caregivers who support them
  • Home and vehicle accessibility accommodations
  • Grants for community and neighborhood projects
  • Education and programming around inclusion and wellbeing
